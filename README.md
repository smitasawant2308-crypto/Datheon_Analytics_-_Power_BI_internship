# Superstore Sales Analysis Using Python

## 1. Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand customer purchasing behaviour, sales performance, product category revenue, monthly sales trends, and the impact of discounts on profitability.

The project uses Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to clean, process, analyze, and visualize sales data.

The main purpose is to identify useful business insights that can help improve sales strategies, customer targeting, pricing decisions, and overall business performance.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchase volume and buying behaviour.
- Identify monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Examine category-wise sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales and profit across different regions.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Generate meaningful business insights.

## 5. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The following data preparation activities were performed:

- Checked missing values.
- Removed records without valid order dates.
- Converted date columns into datetime format.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Converted relevant columns into appropriate data types.
- Extracted Year, Month, Month Name, and Year-Month columns.

## 6. Exploratory Data Analysis

The project includes the following analysis:

**Customer Analysis:** Examines customer segments based on purchase quantity and sales.

**Monthly Sales Analysis:** Identifies monthly sales trends and seasonal variations.

**Category Analysis:** Compares revenue and profit across product categories.

**Regional Analysis:** Examines sales and profit performance across different regions.

**Discount Analysis:** Studies the relationship between discount rates and profit.

**Product Analysis:** Identifies the top 10 products based on sales.

## 7. Key Business Findings

The analysis aims to identify:

1. The product category generating the highest revenue.
2. The most profitable product category.
3. The customer segment contributing the highest sales.
4. The region generating the highest revenue.
5. The months with the strongest sales performance.
6. How different discount levels relate to profitability.

## 8. Business Recommendations

- Improve inventory planning based on monthly sales trends.
- Focus on profitable product categories.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for customer segments.
- Review discount policies to protect profit margins.
- Improve sales strategies in lower-performing regions.
